❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an animated SVG to a 4K MP4 video online for free? ▼
Paste your animated or static SVG code into the live editor or pick one of the presets. Select your target resolution (such as 4K UHD 3840x2160), set your desired frame rate (up to 60 FPS) and duration, configure a background color or gradient, and click "Synthesize 4K MP4". The video is rendered locally in seconds.
Are my SVG graphics uploaded to any external server? ▼
No. All rasterization and video encoding operations occur entirely inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, WebGL, and WebCodecs APIs. Your vector files, assets, and rendered video files never leave your device.
Does this tool support both CSS @keyframes and SMIL SVG animations? ▼
Yes. SVG to 4K MP4 Studio implements a deterministic frame-stepping rasterizer. It injects synchronized negative CSS animation delays and dynamically offsets SMIL animation clocks for each discrete frame timestamp, completely preventing dropped frames, flickering, or animation freezes.
What video resolutions and frame rates are supported? ▼
You can export videos in 4K Ultra HD (3840x2160), 2K Quad HD (2560x1440), 1080p Full HD (1920x1080), 720p HD (1280x720), or arbitrary custom dimensions up to 60 FPS, with bitrates scalable up to 50 Mbps.
